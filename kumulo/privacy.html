Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

In short

NorN Studio collects no data. Kumulo has no account, no server, no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no analytics or advertising SDK.

Your logbook stays on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your private iCloud, which NorN Studio cannot access.

For weather, maps, place names and terrain, your device asks online services (see below, depending on your device), without ever sending them your logbook.

Controller

Kumulo is published by NorN Studio (Yohann Mingam, independent developer), the controller under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Postal address: see the legal notice. Contact: kumulo.app@gmail.com.

What NorN Studio receives

Nothing. Kumulo sends neither your flights, nor your tracks, photos, location or any usage data, to NorN Studio. What the app sends to outside services to show a screen is described below, for each app.

On iPhone and iPad, if you chose in the device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. Apple manages this (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements).

If you write to us, your email address, your message and anything you attach (a flight file, for example) are only used to answer you. They stay in our mailbox (Gmail, Google), and you can ask us to delete them at any time.

Terrain

To draw the terrain under a flight's altitude graph and compute the slope of a place, the app downloads once some public terrain tiles ("Terrain Tiles", hosted by Amazon Web Services) covering the area, each about 7 km across. Neither the track, nor the time, nor the logbook is sent. As with any website, the server sees the device's IP address.

On iPhone and iPad

On your device

Your logbook is kept in the app's private space: flights and tracks, wings and gear, expiry dates, profile and licence card, photos you choose, recordings from the Fly tab, your acceptance of the flight warning (name, date, version); your spots are kept with your settings. The database of known sites (FFVL and OpenStreetMap) is inside the app: browsing it sends nothing. iOS includes it in your backups according to your settings.

iCloud

If your device is signed in to iCloud, your logbook is kept in your private iCloud database (CloudKit) and your settings and spots in iCloud key-value storage, so that it is the same on all your devices. This is your Apple Account: NorN Studio has no access. Without iCloud, everything stays on the device. Apple's privacy policy applies.

Apple services

Apple Weather (WeatherKit): for forecasts, the app sends Apple the positions of your launches, your spots, the places you open and a grid of points on the map shown, rounded to about 100 m. Neither your flights nor their times.

Apple Maps (MapKit): maps, satellite views, place names (from the take-off and landing positions of an imported flight) and search (the text you type).

App Store (StoreKit): purchases go through Apple; Kumulo only knows whether you have a subscription or lifetime access.

These services are provided by Apple, under its own privacy policy.

Device features

Location and barometer: to record a flight in the Fly tab, when you tap "Start recording"; recording continues with the screen locked until "Stop". It is written to a file on the device and nothing is sent. When the app is open, your location also shows you on the map; it stays on the device.

Photos: read on the device to place the photos taken during a flight on its track. Nothing is written to your library and nothing is sent.

Camera: to photograph the pages of a paper logbook; the text is read on the device.

Notifications: gear reminders and wind alerts for your spots, computed and scheduled on the device, with no server.

Widgets and sharing: a short summary is shared with the widgets on the device; a file (image, IGC, PDF, archive) only goes where you choose to share it.

Retention and deletion

Your data stays as long as you keep it. Me › Profile › Settings › Erase the whole logbook erases everything. Deleting the app erases what is on the device; what is in iCloud is deleted in iPhone Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage.

On Android

On your phone

Your logbook is kept in the app's private space: flights and tracks, wings and gear, expiry dates, profile and licence card, cover photos, recordings from the Fly tab, your acceptance of the flight warning (name, date, time, version).

Android backup

If your Google account backup is turned on in Android's settings, it keeps your logbook and your settings (not the tracks or photos, which are too large). This is a Google service, in your account: NorN Studio has no access. When you switch phones, Android's phone-to-phone transfer also takes the tracks and photos.

Kumulo archive

Me › Profile › Switch phones puts your whole logbook in a single file, which you save or send wherever you like. Kumulo never sends it anywhere by itself.

What goes over the internet

Only to show the screen you asked for, over HTTPS, without your name or your logbook:

  • the position of your sites (your launches or a place you searched for) to weather services: MET Norway, GeoSphere Austria, DMI, Bright Sky (Deutscher Wetterdienst) and, in the United States, the National Weather Service;
  • the numbers of the map tiles of the area shown or of the flight to map and terrain services: EOX, IGN, swisstopo, OpenFreeMap, Terrain Tiles (Amazon Web Services);
  • the text you type in the place search, with a position rounded to 10 km, to Photon (komoot).

Like any website, these services see the phone's IP address; MET Norway and DMI keep it in their logs (DMI: up to 5 years). This is why the Google Play listing shows "Approximate location" and "In-app search history": this data goes to these services, never to NorN Studio.

Permissions

Precise location and barometer: only to record a flight in the Fly tab, when you tap "Start recording"; a notification stays visible and recording continues with the screen locked until "Stop". It is written on the phone and nothing is sent. Kumulo does not ask for background location.

Photos: to find, by their date, the photos taken during a flight and place them on its track. You can allow all photos or only some. The photos stay on the phone.

Notifications: for expiry reminders and during a recording.

Purchases

They go through Google Play, under its terms; Kumulo does not see your payment methods. The usage metrics of Google's billing library are switched off in the app.

Delete

Me › Profile › Settings › Erase the whole logbook. Uninstalling Kumulo erases what is on the phone; the Android backup is managed in Android's Google settings.

Your rights

The GDPR gives you the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. As NorN Studio holds no data about you (except an email you may have sent us), you exercise these rights directly in the app. Any question: kumulo.app@gmail.com. You may also contact the data protection authority of your country.

Children

Kumulo is meant for paraglider pilots and collects no data about anyone, children included.

This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which processes IP addresses to serve the pages and keep the service secure. The site has no cookies, no JavaScript and no analytics, and loads nothing from another site.
