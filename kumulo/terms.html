Kumulo for iPhone and iPad

Version of 6 November 2026.

1. Purpose

Kumulo is a flight logbook app for paragliding, published by NorN Studio (contact: kumulo.app@gmail.com). These terms apply to any use of the app. Using Kumulo means accepting these terms; the Fly tab also asks you to expressly accept the warning.

2. Not a flight instrument

Kumulo is in no way a navigation tool, a flight instrument or a measuring instrument. It replaces neither training, nor the pilot's judgement, nor their instruments (vario, certified GPS), nor official sources (weather, airspace, NOTAMs). The device's GPS is not certified and does not count for competitions or records.

3. Use in flight

The pilot does not use Kumulo in flight: their iPhone stays in a closed pocket with the screen locked. In tandem, the passenger may follow the flight on screen, iPhone secured; nothing shown is meant for flying. The pilot alone remains responsible for their flight, its preparation and compliance with the rules of the air.

4. Accuracy of data

Positions, altitudes, speeds, vario readings, distances, weather forecasts (Apple Weather), site names (FFVL) and statistics come from the device's sensors, imported files and third-party services. They may be wrong, late or missing, without warning. The pilot checks any information before using it.

5. Liability

The pilot uses Kumulo at their own risk. To the fullest extent permitted by law, NorN Studio is not liable for direct or indirect damage linked to the use of the app or its data, in particular flight decisions, accidents, data loss or display errors. Nothing in these terms limits any liability that the law does not allow to be limited (in particular for intent or gross negligence, or for personal injury).

6. Personal data

NorN Studio collects no data: the logbook stays on the pilot's device, in their private iCloud and in their Kumulo archive. The acceptance of the warning (name, date, time, version) is kept the same way, with the pilot.

7. Subscription

Purchases go through the App Store, under Apple's terms; flights imported from before Kumulo and school flights are never blocked.

8. Changes

These terms and the warning may change; a new version of the warning must be accepted again in the Fly tab.
