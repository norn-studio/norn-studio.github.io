Support

kumulo.app@gmail.com

For an import problem, please attach the flight file (IGC or GPX) and tell us your device (model, iOS or Android version) and the Kumulo version (Me › Profile › Settings › About). We answer in English, French or German.

Frequently asked questions

How do I import my flights?
From your flight app or vario: Share › Kumulo. Or in Kumulo: + button › Import files, then choose IGC, GPX or ZIP files, or a whole folder. The help page "How do I import my flights?" (+ button) explains it brand by brand.
I want to try it before importing my whole logbook.
Import a single flight (Share › Kumulo from your flight app, or + button › Import files), or add a flight by hand with the + button. You see its page, its numbers and its 3D track straight away. Your first 15 flights are free.
Are my flights on all my devices?

On iPhone and iPad: Yes, through your private iCloud, if both devices use the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on. NorN Studio has no access to your iCloud.

On Android: No: Kumulo does not sync. Your logbook stays on the phone; your Google account's Android backup keeps it (without the tracks and photos), and the Kumulo archive takes all of it to another device.

How do I switch phones?

On iPhone and iPad: Me › Profile › Switch phones › Prepare the archive: your whole logbook fits in a single .kumulo file, to open on the new device.

On Android: Me › Profile › Switch phones › Save (or Share): your whole logbook fits in a single .kumulo file, to open with Kumulo on the new phone. Android's transfer, when you set up the new phone, also takes your logbook.

How much does Kumulo cost?

On iPhone and iPad: Your first 15 flights are free, with every feature. After that: a yearly subscription (€19.99 in the euro area) or lifetime access (€44.99). When you import an old logbook, its 15 most recent flights come in free; the others need Kumulo unlimited. Every flight counts, whatever its type, and without a subscription your flights stay visible and exportable.

On Android: Your first 15 flights are free, with every feature. After that: a yearly subscription or lifetime access, at the price Google Play shows in your currency. When you import an old logbook, its 15 most recent flights come in free; the others need Kumulo unlimited. Every flight counts, whatever its type, and without a subscription your flights stay visible and exportable.

Restore or cancel a purchase

On iPhone and iPad: Me › Profile › Restore purchases. Manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple Account settings (iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions).

On Android: Me › Profile › Restore purchases (with the same Google account). Manage or cancel the subscription in Google Play › your profile picture › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions.

Delete my data

On iPhone and iPad: Me › Profile › Settings › Erase the whole logbook. Deleting the app erases what is on the device; what is in your iCloud is deleted in iPhone Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage › Kumulo.

On Android: Me › Profile › Settings › Erase the whole logbook. Uninstalling Kumulo erases what is on the phone; the Android backup is managed in Android settings › Google › Backup.

Can I use Kumulo in flight?
No. Kumulo is a logbook, not a flight instrument. In flight, your phone stays stowed with the screen locked; only a passenger may follow the screen.
