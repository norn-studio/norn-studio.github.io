Kumulo, the logbook for paraglider pilots

Kumulo keeps all your flights in one place, with nothing to retype: from your flight app or your vario, in one go. Relive them in 3D, follow your progress towards your next licence, get reminded of your gear checks and export your logbook in your federation's format.

Coming soon to the App Store
  • Imports IGC, GPX and ZIP files, even a whole folder; car journeys and duplicates are set aside.
  • Every flight in 2D or in 3D on satellite imagery, with its numbers, graphs and your photos.
  • Stats, the film of your season, your next licence (BHPA, DHV, SHV, FFVL), gear and reminders.
  • Weather for your launches (Apple Weather) and a PDF export in your federation's format.
  • No account, no ads, no data collected. Your first 15 flights are free.

Kumulo is not a flight instrument. It is a logbook: neither a navigation tool nor a measuring instrument, and its GPS is not certified. Your flight decisions are yours alone.
