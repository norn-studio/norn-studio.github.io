AMC1FCL.050

Follows the EASA logbook model

Flare keeps your logbook in the layout EASA sets out in AMC1 FCL.050: the same columns, the same totals, page after page.

  • Every column of the EASA logbook: single-pilot and multi-pilot time, functions, night, IFR, FSTD sessions.
  • Night by the EASA definition, take-offs and landings by day and by night.
  • FCL.060 recency and ORO.FTL.210 flight time limits, worked out for you.
  • A PDF in the EASA layout, totals carried forward, ready to print and sign.

Flare is not certified or approved by EASA, which does not approve logbook apps: it follows the format of AMC1 FCL.050, and your national authority decides whether it accepts an electronic logbook.

Made for the way pilots log

Everything EASA asks you to record, with the tedious parts done for you.

  • Quick flight entry

    Enter the route and block times: night time and day or night take-offs and landings are worked out from the sun’s position, using the EASA definition or your own rule.

  • A cover for every flight

    The runways you took off from and the city you landed in, from real photos. Circuits are drawn on the real runway, local flights on the map, and simulator sessions get their own.

  • Your paper logbook, by photo

    Photograph each page in landscape. Flare tests its reading before keeping the photo, learns your handwriting from a few boxes you confirm, and shows the box from your photo next to any value it doubts. Everything is read on your device.

  • Recency, limits, expiry dates

    FCL.060 recency for each aircraft type, ORO.FTL.210 flight time limits, and reminders before your medical, ratings and checks expire.

  • Import from other apps

    Bring your history over from a CSV or Excel file, or from an EASA-format PDF logbook. Columns are recognised for you and totals are checked.

  • Airline roster

    Import your roster from your iPhone’s calendar or from an .ics file: the flights you have flown are added, ready for you to complete.

  • EASA PDF and CSV export

    A print-ready EASA logbook in PDF, A4 landscape, and a CSV export of all your flights.

  • Flight map

    All your routes and airports on a map. The airport database is built into the app.

  • Your logbook in pictures

    A passport of your airports and countries, a hangar of every aircraft you flew, your career step by step, your records and your year in flight, ready to share.

  • Simulator sessions

    FSTD sessions with their exercise (OPC, LPC, EBT…), examiner and role, in their own column: never counted as flight time.

  • Filters and totals

    Filter your logbook and see the totals of exactly the flights shown.

  • Flight time calculator

    Add and subtract times in hours and minutes or in decimal hours, and read the total both ways.

  • Widgets and badge

    Total time, recency and your next expiry date on the Home Screen and Lock Screen; the app icon badge counts what needs your attention.

  • Animated guides

    Short animated guides show each gesture in the app, in your language and in its day or night theme.

  • iPhone, iPad, five languages

    One app for iPhone and iPad, in English, French, German, Spanish and Italian.
