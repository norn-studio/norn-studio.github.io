Privacy policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026

In short

  • Flare has no account and no server run by the developer. To illustrate your flights, the app downloads public photos, giving only the aircraft’s registration or the airport (see “Flight photos”).
  • There are no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no third-party SDKs.
  • Your logbook is stored on your device. If you use iCloud sync, it is stored in your own private iCloud database, which the developer cannot access.
  • The developer collects no personal data about you.

Who is responsible

Flare is published by Yohann Mingam, an individual developer, who is the data controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Postal address: see the legal notice. Contact: norn.studio.apps@gmail.com.

What the developer collects

Nothing. The app has no account and no sign-in, and the developer runs no server for it. It sends neither your logbook, nor your photos, nor your calendar, nor any usage data to the developer or to anyone else. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code and no third-party SDKs, and it does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

If you have chosen, in your iPhone’s settings, to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide the developer with crash reports and anonymous, aggregated usage statistics. This is managed by Apple, and you can change it at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Data stored on your device

Your logbook is kept in the app’s private storage on your device (its sandbox): your flights, the crew names you type, aircraft, expiry dates and your profile (name, licence number, roles, company). iOS includes it in your backups according to your own settings for iCloud Backup or for backups to a computer.

iCloud sync

iCloud sync is optional. In the versions of the app that offer it, you turn it on or off in Settings › Data › Sync with iCloud.

When it is on, your logbook is stored in your own private iCloud database (CloudKit), operated by Apple under your Apple Account, so that it is shared between your devices. The developer has no access to it. Your profile and a few settings (night rule, duration format, export options) are kept in step through iCloud key-value storage. Apple’s privacy policy applies to iCloud.

When you turn sync off, your data stays on your device. What is already in iCloud stays there until you delete it (see “Keeping and deleting your data”).

Device features the app uses

  • Camera: used only to photograph the pages of your paper logbook. The text is recognised on your device by Apple’s Vision framework. The photos are not sent anywhere and are not kept once reading is done.
  • Photos: you choose images with the system photo picker. The app receives only the images you pick and has no access to your photo library.
  • Calendar: the app asks for full access only if you start a roster import from your calendar. Events are read on your device to find your flights; once you have chosen your roster calendars, the app reads them again on your device when you open it, to suggest newly flown flights. Nothing is written to your calendar and nothing is sent. You can withdraw access at any time in your iPhone’s Settings.
  • Files: the documents you choose to import or export (PDF, CSV, Excel, .ics) are processed on your device. An exported file goes only where you choose to save or share it.
  • Notifications: reminders of expiry dates and recency are local notifications scheduled on your device, with no push server. The app icon badge is also set on the device.
  • Widget: to show your figures on the Home Screen and Lock Screen, the app writes a small summary (total time, recency, next expiry date…) to a container on your device that it shares with its widget.

Map

The flight map is drawn by Apple Maps (MapKit): your device loads the map tiles from Apple, and Apple’s privacy policy applies to them. The airport database is built into the app: looking up an airport sends nothing.

Flight photos

To illustrate your flights (flight detail, flight entry, passport, hangar, widgets), the app downloads public photos: the aircraft’s photo from Planespotters.net, using its registration; the city’s photo from Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons, using the name of the airport, its city or its coordinates; the satellite view of the runways from Apple Maps.

Only this public information is sent, with no identifier: not your logbook, not your flight dates, not your name. Like any website, these services receive your device’s IP address in order to respond; their own privacy policies apply.

The photos are kept in the app’s cache on your device (100 MB at most), never in your logbook or in iCloud. Settings › Flight photos lets you turn these downloads off, limit them to Wi-Fi or empty the cache.

Purchases

Flare Pro is sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer never sees your payment details and receives from Apple only anonymous, aggregated sales reports. The app asks the App Store (StoreKit) for the status of your subscription and keeps the last known status on your device (end of the paid period, renewal) so that it works without a network.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept for as long as you keep it. To delete it:

  • in the app: Settings › Data › Erase the whole logbook;
  • deleting the app deletes its data from your device (copies in your backups follow your backup settings);
  • data in iCloud: in your iPhone’s Settings › [your name] › iCloud, open the storage management, choose Flare and delete its data.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify and erase your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability.

As the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to hand over, correct or delete on the developer’s side. Any request will be answered by explaining how to view, export (PDF or CSV), correct or delete your data in the app and in iCloud. Write to norn.studio.apps@gmail.com.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular the one of the EU country where you live or work.

Writing to the developer

If you write by email, your address and your message are used only to answer you.

Children

Flare is a tool for pilots and is not directed at children.

This website

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads nothing from third parties: its fonts and images are served by the site itself. It is hosted by GitHub, Inc., which, like any web host, processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver the pages and keep the service secure.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. A change in how the app handles your data, for example a new feature, will be described here when the version that brings it is released.

Contact

For any question about this policy or your data: norn.studio.apps@gmail.com.
