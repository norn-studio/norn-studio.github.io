Privacy policy for Android

Version of 26 November 2026

In short

  • Flare has no account, and the developer runs no server for it. Your logbook, your photos and your calendar are never sent to the developer.
  • Your logbook is stored on your phone. If Android backup is on, Android also copies it to your own Google account.
  • The developer has added no advertising, no tracking and no analytics.
  • The app does contain Google components for reading text, showing maps and handling purchases. They send technical data to Google, described below. Google Maps and ML Kit start only with your consent, which you can withdraw in the app’s settings.

Who is responsible

Flare is published by NorN Studio, the business name of Yohann Mingam, who is the data controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Postal address: see the legal notice. Contact: norn.studio.apps@gmail.com.

What the developer receives

The app itself sends the developer nothing: no flights, no profile, no crew names, no photos, no calendar events and no usage data. As the publisher of an app on Google Play, the developer does receive the following from Google:

  • Statistics: figures on installs, uninstalls, ratings and countries;
  • Technical reports: crash and freeze reports, if you allow your phone to share usage and diagnostic data with Google;
  • Sales reports: for each order, its number, date, product, price, tax and country. They contain no payment details;
  • Reviews: what you publish on Google Play, under the name shown there.

Sales records are kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires.

Data stored on your phone

Your logbook is kept in the app’s private storage: your flights, the crew names you type, aircraft, simulators, expiry dates, your profile (name, licence number, roles, company) and the photos you add to your aircraft and simulators. The app also keeps your settings, the corrections it remembers from reading your paper logbook, the calendars you chose, the roster flights already offered to you and the last known status of your purchases. Widgets keep their own copy of figures and images from your logbook to show them on your home screen.

Backups

Android backup. If backup is on in your phone’s settings, Android copies your logbook, profile, crew, fleet, expiry dates, settings and your own aircraft and simulator photos to a private area of your Google account, and to a new phone when you transfer your data. The copy also includes the paper logbook reading corrections, the calendars you chose, the roster flights already offered to you and the last known status of your purchases. The developer has no access to it. According to Google, the copy is end-to-end encrypted on Android 9 or later using your phone’s PIN, pattern or password, and is limited to 25 MB per app, your own photos included. Google’s privacy policy applies.

Backup file. Settings › Data › Backup › Export a backup writes your logbook, your profile and all your settings to a file, in the place you choose. Your photos are not included. This file is not encrypted: keep it somewhere safe.

Phone features the app uses

  • Camera: to photograph the pages of your paper logbook and, if you wish, your aircraft or simulators. Photos of logbook pages stay in memory during the reading and are not kept afterwards.
  • Photos: you choose images with Android’s photo picker. The app receives only the images you pick.
  • Calendar: read only, and requested only if you import your roster from a calendar. Events are read on your phone to find your flights. Once you have chosen calendars, the app reads the last 30 days again when the home screen opens, to suggest flights you have flown. Nothing is written to your calendar, and its events are not sent.
  • Files: the documents you import or export (PDF, CSV, Excel, .ics, backup) are processed on your phone and go only where you choose. PDF and CSV exports and the images you share are first prepared in the app’s private cache.
  • Notifications: reminders are scheduled on your phone, and scheduled again after it restarts.
  • Location: the app does not ask for your position.

Google components in the app

Only with your consent. Google Maps and ML Kit start only once you have agreed in the app, which asks you before their first use: before the map and the satellite views for Google Maps, when you open the paper logbook reader for ML Kit. Without your consent, the map is drawn by the app itself, flight covers have no satellite view and the paper logbook reader is not available. You can withdraw your consent at any time in Settings › Google services, with two switches: “Maps and satellite views (Google Maps)” and “Paper logbook reading (ML Kit)”.

Reading your paper logbook (ML Kit). The text is recognised on your phone: the pictures of your pages are not sent. ML Kit is part of the app and sends Google technical data for diagnostics and usage analytics: device model and Android version, the app’s name and version, a per-installation identifier, performance figures, image format and size, events and error codes. According to Google, these data are encrypted in transit and not passed to third parties. Details from Google.

Map and satellite views (Google Maps). Your phone loads the map images of the areas you view from Google. Google receives your IP address, device and SDK information, a pseudonymous identifier specific to the Maps SDK, crash data and your interactions with the map, such as panning and zooming. Your routes and flights are drawn by the app on top of the map and are not sent. Details from Google.

These components are provided by Google Ireland Limited, which may also process data outside the European Union. Google’s privacy policy applies.

Flight photos

To illustrate your flights, the app downloads public photos: the aircraft’s photo from Planespotters.net, using its registration; the city’s photo from Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons, using the name of the airport, its city or its coordinates; the satellite view of the runways from Google Maps (only if you have agreed to Google Maps, see above).

Only this public information is sent, not your logbook, your flight dates or your name. These services receive your phone’s IP address in order to respond, and their own privacy policies apply.

Downloaded photos are kept in the app’s cache (100 MB at most), never in your logbook or its backup. Satellite views stay in memory, but an image you choose to share may contain one. Settings › Flight photos lets you turn these downloads off, limit them to Wi-Fi or empty the cache. Images already prepared for widgets are stored separately and are not removed when you empty the cache.

Purchases

Flare Pro is purchased through Google Play. Google handles the payment, and the developer never sees your payment details. The app asks Google Play for the status of your purchases and keeps the last known status on your phone, so that it works without a network.

Legal bases

Data are processed to provide the features you use and the purchases you make (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR), to meet tax and accounting obligations (Article 6(1)(c)), and on the basis of the legitimate interest in keeping the app reliable, understanding faults and answering you (Article 6(1)(f)).

Google Maps and ML Kit are used only with your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR); storing and reading information on your phone for them relies on that same consent (Section 25(1) TDDDG, the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act). Withdrawing it, in Settings › Google services, applies to the future and does not affect the lawfulness of what was done before.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept for as long as you keep it. To delete it:

  • in the app: Settings › Data › Erase the whole logbook deletes your flights and, if you choose, your crew, fleet, simulators and expiry dates. Your profile, settings, reading corrections and own photos remain;
  • uninstalling the app deletes all its data from your phone;
  • Android backups are managed in your phone’s settings and in your Google account;
  • any backup files you exported must be deleted by you.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify and erase your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. Write to norn.studio.apps@gmail.com. For data processed by Google, you can also use the tools in your Google account.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular the one of the EU country where you live or work.

Writing to the developer

If you write by email, your address and your message are used only to answer you.

Children

Flare is a tool for pilots and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact
