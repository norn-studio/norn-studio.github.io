Who provides the app

What the app does

Flare is a pilot logbook that follows the format of AMC1 FCL.050. It helps you record flights and calculates figures such as night time, totals, recency and flight time limits. No account is needed, and your logbook is stored on your phone. Flare is not certified or approved by EASA or by any other authority.

Your responsibility as a pilot

You remain responsible for the content of your logbook and for meeting the rules that apply to you. Calculated or imported values, including values read from photos, are aids: check them before relying on them. Flare is not a flight planning, navigation or operational decision tool. Ask your authority or operator if you are unsure whether an electronic logbook is accepted.

Free version and Flare Pro

The free version lets you add up to 50 flights. Simulator sessions and positioning do not count towards this limit. Flare Pro removes the limit, either as a yearly subscription or as lifetime access paid once. The features and prices are shown in the app and on Google Play before you buy.

Without Flare Pro, you can still view, search, correct and export your whole logbook, whatever its size.
